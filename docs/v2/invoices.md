## Confirmed owner decisions

- The owner expects to invoice customers worldwide, mostly in Germany.
- The owner has chosen to continue planning and building their own V2 invoicing system rather than replace it with Lexware Office. V2 is intended to be the single issuer/source of real invoices for this workflow; do not add a Lexware integration or create duplicate official invoices by default. Keep the design adaptable to future business changes without rewriting already issued records. This choice does not waive German tax, retention, e-invoice, or payment-compliance review before real use.
- The owner currently expects to operate as a German `Kleinunternehmer`; the actual registered tax status and invoice rules must be verified before issuing real invoices. Do not encode an automatic switch to 19% tax from a guessed sales threshold. Turnover is not profit, and cross-border treatment can differ by transaction.
- Scope includes one-time invoices, installment arrangements (including examples such as 50% / 30% / 20%), and monthly or yearly subscriptions. The owner-facing workflows are agreed below; the legally correct document lifecycle for advance/final invoices must be verified before implementation.
- Every invoice belongs to a Client in V2. The invoice flow must allow quick creation of a Client if needed. No invoice for an unregistered customer.
- Workflow: save draft, preview, explicitly issue, then choose whether to send or download. Never send automatically simply because an invoice is issued.
- Initial payment methods are bank transfer and Stripe, with manually recorded cash payments. A separate PayPal integration is out of initial scope unless the owner explicitly adds it later.
- Legacy invoices were test data only. No migration of them to V2 is required; do not delete the legacy system merely because migration is unnecessary.
- Initial currency selection includes EUR and USD; other currencies are future scope. Exchange-rate pricing and tax reporting remain distinct implementation checks below.
- Invoice language selection includes German and English, with Arabic copies requested by the owner. All language copies must identify the same invoice rather than issue a second bill. Confirm the exact legal/document format before producing real multilingual invoices.
- For installments, the owner prefers one simple customer-facing invoice/payment-plan workflow that can contain any number of payments (for example 2, 3, 5, or 6). Whether advance payments require distinct legally correct advance/final invoice documents must be resolved before implementation; do not assume one PDF suffices in every case.
- Each subscription chooses a collection mode. Manual mode prepares a monthly/yearly invoice draft for owner review and explicit issue/send, with the customer paying by bank transfer or a one-time Stripe link. Automatic-card mode uses Stripe with the customer's explicit saved-card consent, charges on schedule, and automatically issues/sends the corresponding invoice without monthly owner review. For either mode, there must be exactly one authoritative issued invoice per billed period, kept privately in Media and included in the year's export; never send duplicate invoice documents or mark a charge as paid before payment confirmation. The exact Stripe/V2 invoice-of-record integration is still a material implementation/legal decision.
- Only the owner creates a subscription after agreeing terms with the customer; the public site has no self-service subscription purchase flow in this scope. A secure payment-method collection step for an owner-created automatic-card subscription is not a public purchase flow.
- Owner-approved changes to an existing subscription's price or discount take effect from the next billing period only, never retroactively or as an unreviewed mid-period charge. Previously issued invoices retain their original terms.
- A customer requests cancellation by contacting the owner; the owner reviews and performs cancellation in the Dashboard. There is no customer self-service cancellation action in this scope. Before offering consumer subscriptions in Germany, review whether this process satisfies applicable cancellation rules for the actual way contracts are concluded; this planning choice is not a legal conclusion.
- Bank-transfer payments may be recorded manually; Stripe payments should be recorded only after actual payment confirmation. The owner also wants to handle cash, so payment methods and partial-payment records must not be limited to bank/Stripe.
- Sending an issued invoice opens a V2 Inbox draft with the invoice attached for owner review and an explicit send action.
- The owner wants the finalized invoice PDF saved privately in shared Media, organized in a folder such as `Invoices/2026`, before attaching it to the Inbox draft. This extends the initial Media module's upload-from-computer-only workflow and requires an approved generated-file integration. Moving/renaming the folder must not break invoice references, and an issued invoice's retained file must not be deletable like an ordinary unused asset.
- The owner currently operates from Germany and wants a German invoice version as the baseline, with an English or Arabic copy when a client needs one. These copies must not accidentally become separate billable invoices; the exact translation/document policy remains subject to legal review.
- In an installment plan, the owner wants to freely choose each installment amount and due date, rather than fixed percentages or milestones. The total and remaining balance must stay consistent.
- Seller details, including deliberately fake details for testing, should be editable in Dashboard Settings. The owner wants to exercise the complete invoice flow before their real business setup is ready. The owner approved a separate test mode: clearly marked `TEST` documents, separate test numbering, test email/payment path, and no confusion with real invoices or live Stripe charges. The precise controls and full end-to-end test path still require specification.
- The owner wants customers to be able to choose bank transfer or Stripe when both are offered. For the first version, show bank details and a Stripe payment link; QR codes are deferred.
- After a manual cash payment, offer an on-demand receipt, not an automatically generated receipt for every payment.
- Provide an owner-only year-selectable download action for the owner's future tax adviser, e.g. `Download 2026 invoices`. It downloads a private package to the owner's computer; it does not automatically email anyone. Include real issued, cancelled, and corrected invoice documents plus payment records; exclude drafts and test-mode data. The owner has no tax adviser or requested adviser-specific format yet, so start with PDFs and CSV records in one downloadable archive and leave specialist export formats for later. The exact accounting-year grouping and translated-copy layout still need specification.
- An issued invoice is not silently rewritten after a mistake. Retain the original and create a linked correction or cancellation document, with the exact legally appropriate flow reviewed before implementation.
- If an invoice that received payment is cancelled, show the amount potentially due back and allow the owner to record the actual refund amount, date, and method. Cancellation must not silently mark a refund as completed.
- Subscriptions support pause, resume, and end; these actions control future periods/drafts and do not rewrite previously issued invoices.
- The owner wants to select a relevant offered Service as a starting point for subscription pricing, or enter a custom agreement when no Service fits. Record a historical name/price snapshot on the subscription rather than making its agreed terms depend on later Services edits; `docs/v2/services.md` keeps Services independent. No automatic repricing.
- A subscription may use a matching Service as a starting point, but when no suitable Service exists the owner can enter a special agreed price for that customer. A change to a Service's public price is clearly intended for new customers; whether an existing customer's subscription price changes is decided case by case by the owner, never silently by a catalogue edit.
- When an invoice or subscription uses USD while the Services catalogue is priced in EUR, the owner wants an automatic conversion proposal that they can review and override before issue. The agreed USD amount is fixed for an existing subscription until the owner explicitly changes it; future periods do not recalculate it from a changing exchange rate. The source/date, rounding, and rate-unavailable fallback remain open. A conversion used for customer pricing is not automatically the same as the exchange rate required for tax reporting.
- Discounts can be percentage-based or fixed-amount, on one-time invoices and subscriptions. A subscription discount may apply to one period, a selected number of periods, or indefinitely. Public Service promotions never automatically change an existing customer's subscription agreement.
- A subscription may be free temporarily or indefinitely. Free periods are recorded on the subscription in the Dashboard; no zero-value invoice is generated by default, subject to legal/accounting review. For a time-limited free period followed by automatic card collection, obtain explicit customer consent and card details through Stripe at the beginning, clearly show the future amount and first charge date, and send a reminder seven days before the first charge. A permanently free subscription does not require a card. If the card is missing or invalid when a trial ends, notify both parties and wait for an updated card without silently switching payment methods or automatically cancelling the subscription.
- If an automatic card charge fails, mark the period as overdue rather than paid and notify the owner and customer. When Stripe permits, retry automatically up to two additional times and send three customer reminders over 14 days. Do not mark an unsuccessful attempt paid or automatically end the subscription after retries are exhausted; the owner decides whether and when to cancel. Verify that Stripe's actual recovery settings cannot override this policy by automatically cancelling the subscription.
- The owner chooses the first collection date per subscription. Monthly billing keeps the selected day where it exists; if the selected day is the 29th, 30th, or 31st and a later month lacks it, use that month's last day.
- If a time-limited free period ends without a valid card, notify the owner and customer, wait for an updated card, and do not silently switch to bank collection. The owner may cancel a subscription manually after multiple reminders if the customer still does not pay; no automatic cancellation merely because a card failed.

## Engineering decisions (Backend2, 23–24 Sep 2026)

Made by the implementation agent while the owner was away, under the explicit instruction to build Backend2 test-mode-first. Each one is reversible in code; none of them is a legal conclusion. Change any of them by telling the next agent.

- **Money.** Integer minor units (cents) plus a currency, EUR or USD only. Nothing ever sums across currencies — every report is per currency. Rounding is half away from zero, once per line (`quantity × unit price`, quantity in thousandths) and once per tax group. An invoice-level discount (percent in basis points, or a fixed amount capped at the subtotal) is spread across tax rates by largest remainder so the parts add up exactly. Unit prices are **net**.
- **Seller settings** live in the database (one row), editable in Invoice settings: name, address, country, email, phone, website, tax number, VAT id, bank holder/IBAN/BIC/bank name, tax mode, default rate, payment terms, default language, and an optional *test recipient* address. Tax mode `kleinunternehmer` (default: 0 % and the § 19 UStG sentence) or `standard` (per-line 19/7/0 %, default 19 %, optional reverse charge which needs the customer's VAT id). No automatic threshold switching, ever.
- **Readiness.** A live invoice is refused (`SELLER_NOT_READY`, with the missing fields) unless name, address, email, tax number *or* VAT id, IBAN and account holder are filled. Test mode needs none of it.
- **Test mode vs live mode.** Every invoice and subscription is `test` or `live`. Test documents carry a `TEST` watermark and banner, number as `TEST-2026-0001` from their own counter, go to the test recipient when one is set, have `[TEST]` in the email subject, and are excluded from the yearly export and (by default) from analytics. **Live issuing, live cancellation documents and live card charges are refused unless `INVOICES_LIVE_ENABLED=true`** — unset everywhere tonight, so nothing real could be issued.
- **Numbering.** `2026-0001` (live) / `TEST-2026-0001` (test): gapless per mode per calendar year (Berlin issue date). The number is taken only at issue, inside the issuing transaction, from a counter row locked `FOR UPDATE`; everything that can refuse runs before it, and a rollback returns it. A unique index on `(mode, year, seq)` is the last line. Cancellation documents take their number from the same sequence. Drafts have no number and can be deleted freely.
- **Lifecycle.** Draft (editable, may be incomplete; the Dashboard gets `issueProblems`) → preview (PDF marked DRAFT, no number) → issue (needs the revision the owner previewed; idempotent; freezes an immutable `snapshot` of seller, recipient, lines, totals, tax note, installments, language) → send (an Inbox draft with the stored PDF attached; the owner presses Send) or download. Issue date = today in Berlin (no back-dating). Service date defaults to the issue date when left empty. Due date = issue date + payment terms, or the last installment date.
- **Recipient.** Clients have no billing address, so each invoice carries recipient fields (name, company, address, country, email, VAT id), prefilled from the Client's most recent invoice, else from the Client. Quick-create of a Client goes through the Clients module (its duplicate warning included) in the same transaction.
- **Service snapshot.** A line (or a subscription) may start from a Service: its name and price (the published price if any, else the draft's; catalogue EUR) are copied once and never re-read. No key to Services.
- **Installments.** Any number (up to 60) of freely dated amounts on one invoice, which must add up to the total exactly and move forward in time. Payments cover installments oldest first; each installment shows paid / partially paid / open / overdue.
- **Payments.** Methods bank, stripe, cash, other. Manual entries (bank, cash, other) carry an idempotency key; overpayment by hand is refused (`PAYMENT_TOO_LARGE`); dates may not be in the future. A mistaken manual payment is voided with a reason, never deleted. Card payments are recorded **only** from a verified Stripe webhook, idempotent by PaymentIntent — and money that really arrived is always recorded (surplus shows as refundable). Payment state (unpaid / partially paid / paid / overdue / cancelled) is derived on every read from Berlin's today; overdue = something due before today is unpaid, or an automatic charge failed.
- **Cash receipt.** On demand, for cash payments only, rendered once and stored in Media like the invoice.
- **Corrections.** Issued documents never change. Cancel = a numbered cancellation document (Stornorechnung) reversing every line, pointing back at the original; the original is marked cancelled and keeps its PDF. Correct = cancel + one new linked draft copied from the original (asking twice returns the same draft). A cancelled invoice that received money shows the refundable amount; refunds are recorded by the owner (amount, date, method), never assumed; a refund can never exceed what was kept. Payments stay on the original invoice (moving them to the correction is an open question below).
- **Documents and Media.** PDFs are made with pdf-lib + fontkit and the site's own Space Grotesk (SIL OFL, embedded and subset; the base64 font copied into `modules/invoices/invoice.font.ts` because Backend2 imports nothing from the legacy backend). German is the baseline; an English copy prints "English copy of invoice N — same invoice, not an additional bill" and shares the number. Every stored PDF goes to Media under `Invoices/<year>` (the root folder is remembered by id, so the owner may rename or move it), with a Media `record` reference from Invoices **and** a `RESTRICT` key from `v2_invoice_files` — Media refuses to delete it (`DELETE_BLOCKED_BY_REFERENCES`). Storing happens after the issue commits; if storage fails the document is rendered from the snapshot on first download.
- **Arabic copies: not supported yet, and refused honestly** (`LANGUAGE_NOT_SUPPORTED`). fontkit does shape Arabic, but the only Arabic font in the repository (Readex Pro, variable WOFF2) crashes pdf-lib's font subsetter, mixed right-to-left/left-to-right lines (numbers, IBANs, Latin names) would need a separate bidi pass, and a wrong-looking Arabic legal document is worse than none. Enabling it needs an open-licensed static Arabic TTF (e.g. Noto Naskh Arabic) and a reviewed RTL layout.
- **Sending.** One Inbox draft per invoice (reused while unsent), addressed to the recipient's email (or the test recipient in test mode), with the PDF attached and — when card payment is allowed — a Stripe Checkout link for the amount still due in the text. Nothing is sent by being issued; only automatic-card subscription invoices are sent by themselves (the owner chose no monthly review for those). Inbox's own `INBOX_SEND_MODE` still decides whether any email really leaves.
- **Stripe.** A thin `fetch` adapter (no SDK; Worker-compatible) with an in-memory fake for tests. Only Checkout (payment links and card-saving in `setup` mode) and off-session PaymentIntents that V2 schedules are used — **not** Stripe Billing or Stripe Invoicing — so Stripe never sends a competing invoice and its recovery settings cannot cancel anything. A `sk_test_` key serves test-mode documents only; a `sk_live_` key serves live ones only and is refused unless `INVOICES_LIVE_ENABLED=true`. Every call carries an idempotency key (same invoice + same balance → same Checkout session; one key per charge attempt). Return URLs are the public site root with `?payment=complete|cancelled` (a thank-you page needs design approval).
- **Webhook.** `POST /api/v2/stripe/webhook`: `Stripe-Signature` HMAC-SHA256 over `timestamp.rawBody`, constant-time compare, 5-minute tolerance; off entirely without `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Each event id is recorded once in the same transaction as its effect (duplicates change nothing; a failed effect rolls back so Stripe retries). The event's `livemode` must match the document's mode. Handled: `checkout.session.completed` / `async_payment_succeeded` (payment, or a saved card), `payment_intent.succeeded` / `payment_failed` (subscription charges).
- **Subscriptions.** Monthly or yearly, billed in advance on the first day of each period; the start date's day is the anchor (29/30/31 fall back to a shorter month's last day and return afterwards; 29 Feb yearly → 28 Feb). Manual collection: the job prepares one draft per period for the owner. Automatic card: the job issues the invoice, sends it, and schedules the charge. One authoritative invoice per period is guaranteed by the period ledger's primary key and a unique index on invoices. Every change takes a date **on or after today** and so reaches only periods not yet decided: pause (periods starting in the pause are skipped, not billed later), resume, end (default: the current period runs out), price change (default: the next period; must be a period start), discount (percent or fixed, for 1 / N / unlimited **invoiced** periods — free periods do not use it up), free ranges (no zero invoice). Never cancelled automatically.
- **Failed and missing cards.** A failed charge makes the invoice overdue (never paid), schedules retry 2 at +3 days and retry 3 at +7 days after the first failure (two additional attempts, no more), notifies the owner once, and prepares three customer reminders (days 0, 7, 14) as Inbox drafts. A missing or invalid card: the charge waits (`waiting_for_card`), the invoice shows overdue, both sides get a notice, no switch to bank transfer; once a card is saved the charge runs on the next job. After a free start, the customer is reminded 7 days before the first charge. Reminders are prepared as Inbox drafts for the owner to send; switching them to automatic sending is a small follow-up.
- **EUR → USD.** The ECB daily reference rate (free, no key), fetched at most every 6 hours, stored with its date and source; the owner can type a rate instead (stored as `manual`). Unavailable → the most recent ECB rate up to 7 days old, labelled with its date; older → `FX_UNAVAILABLE` and the owner enters one. `USD = round(EUR × rate)`. It is a **proposal** for customer pricing — the rate id travels with the invoice/subscription as an audit trail — never the tax-reporting rate. A subscription's USD price stays fixed until the owner changes it.
- **Yearly export.** `GET /owner/invoices/exports/:year` → a ZIP of live invoice, cancelled-invoice and cancellation PDFs (baseline language) plus `documents.csv`, `payments.csv`, `refunds.csv`. Documents group by issue-date year, payments/refunds by the year they happened. Drafts and test mode are excluded by the query. CSV cells are guarded against spreadsheet formulas. Nothing is emailed.
- **Clock.** Every "today" is Berlin's calendar day from one clock (`invoice.clock.ts`), which the tests move across month ends, leap years and both daylight-saving changes.

## Open legal and business questions (not resolved by the build)

1. **Advance invoices (Anzahlungsrechnungen).** The plan is implemented as a payment schedule on one invoice issued up front. Whether payments requested before the work is complete need separate advance invoices and a final invoice (§ 14 (5) UStG) must be checked with a tax adviser before real use.
2. **E-invoicing.** From 2025/2027 German B2B invoices move to structured e-invoices (XRechnung/ZUGFeRD). Kleinunternehmer are exempt from *issuing* them, but a switch to standard taxation would require them. Not built.
3. **Retention.** Stored PDFs are locked against ordinary deletion but the 8/10-year retention period, GoBD-compliant archiving and an immutable audit trail beyond the event log are not certified.
4. **Foreign currency and tax.** A USD invoice under standard taxation would need the VAT amount also in EUR at the official rate (§ 16 (6) UStG); not implemented (Kleinunternehmer shows no VAT, so not needed today).
5. **Correcting a paid invoice.** Payments stay on the cancelled original and show as refundable; whether they should instead be transferred to the correction is an owner decision.
6. **Multilingual documents.** Which language copy is legally authoritative, and the Arabic copy (see above).
7. **Automatic subscriptions for consumers** — cancellation rules, and the consent wording on Stripe's card page.
8. **Stripe return page** — Checkout returns to the public site root until a page is designed and approved.

## Analytics interface (read-only, `modules/invoices/invoice.analytics.ts`)

All take an optional `includeTest` (default `false` = live only; the answer reports `includesTest`). Money is per currency; cancellation documents are never revenue. Call inside a request or `runWithDb`.

- `receivedPayments({ from, to, includeTest? })` → `{ includesTest, received, refunded, net }`, each `Array<{ currency: 'EUR'|'USD', amountMinor }>`; payments by `paid_on`, refunds by `refunded_on`, inclusive Berlin dates, voided payments excluded.
- `overdueInvoices({ includeTest? })` → `{ includesTest, byCurrency: Array<{ currency, count, amountDueMinor }> }` as of Berlin's today.
- `invoiceStatusMix({ from?, to?, includeTest? })` → `{ includesTest, drafts, states: { unpaid, partially_paid, paid, overdue, cancelled } }`, optionally only invoices issued in the range.
- `subscriptionCounts({ includeTest? })` → `{ includesTest, active, paused, ended, byCollection: { manual, automatic_card } }` (collection counts exclude ended ones).
