# Project

## What this is

A personal platform for one person: Yaman Warda, a solo developer in Erfurt,
Germany, selling websites, Shopify stores, and custom business systems.

It has two halves that share one codebase, one origin, and one database:

1. **A public site** that presents the offer, the work, and the person, and
   converts visitors into booked calls and messages.
2. **A private admin** that runs the business behind it: content, leads,
   bookings, clients, projects, invoices, payments, subscriptions, referrals,
   and an analytics overview.

## Who it serves

| Audience | Where they land | What they need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small business owners in Germany | `/`, `/services`, `/work` | Understand what can be built for them, then book a call |
| Companies considering hiring | `/stack`, `/about` | Judge technical depth and working style |
| Readers | `/blog` | Opinions and thinking worth following |
| The owner | `/admin` | Run every part of the business in one place |

The two client-facing messages stay separate. `/` speaks to buyers of services.
`/stack` speaks to employers. Mixing them weakens both.

## Why build it instead of buying tools

Three reasons, in order of weight:

1. Everything lives in one admin instead of five tabs and five subscriptions.
2. It is the strongest portfolio piece available — a complete system, built and
   operated by its author, shown as a case study on the site it powers.
3. The data is owned, on a German server, which makes the GDPR story simple for
   client records and invoices.

## What it is not

Read the Scope Boundary in `AGENTS.md`. The short version: no SaaS, no second
user, no page builder, no mail client, no accounting system, no client portal.

## Success conditions

The platform is working when:

- A stranger can understand the offer, see proof, and book a call without email.
- Content changes do not require opening the codebase.
- Every lead, booking, client, and invoice is visible from one dashboard.
- The owner knows monthly revenue and outstanding invoices at a glance.
- The platform itself can be shown to a client as evidence of capability.

## Constraints

- Solo operator, low volume. Design for one person, not for scale.
- German legal context for invoicing and data protection.
- Trilingual public surface: German, English, Arabic, including RTL.
- Built through AI-assisted development in small reviewed slices, by a developer
  who understands and reviews what is produced.
